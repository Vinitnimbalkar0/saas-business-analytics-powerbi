# Business Problem

## RavenStack SaaS Analytics

RavenStack is a synthetic SaaS business scenario covering revenue, subscriptions, customer accounts, product usage, customer support, and churn.

The core business problem is that these operational domains exist separately. Management needs a consolidated, business-ready view to understand recurring revenue, customer segmentation, subscription behavior, product engagement, support workload, and customer churn.

## Key Business Questions

- How much recurring revenue is being generated and how is it changing over time?
- Which plans, regions, industries, and customer segments contribute most to revenue?
- How many customers are active, churned, trial, or paid?
- Where is churn concentrated and which reasons or customer journeys are associated with it?
- Which product features generate the most usage and errors?
- How much support demand is generated and which priorities or accounts create the largest workload?
- How do support volume and service performance relate to churn?

## Analytics Objective

Build an end-to-end analytics solution that transforms raw SaaS operational data into a reporting-ready model and Power BI dashboard for business decision support.

## Business Value

The solution connects:

**Revenue → Customers → Product Engagement → Support → Churn**

This allows business users to investigate performance by plan, geography, industry, customer segment, feature, support priority, and customer journey.
